Details of college

CHARUSAT (Charotar University of Science and Technology) is a renowned educational institution located in the Charotar region of Gujarat, India. It was established with the aim of providing quality education in various fields of science, technology, and management. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across disciplines such as engineering, pharmacy, management, computer applications, and more. CHARUSAT is known for its state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and emphasis on research and innovation. The university also promotes industry-academia collaboration and encourages students to participate in extracurricular activities to foster holistic development.
